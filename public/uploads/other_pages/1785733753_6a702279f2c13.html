Course Overview

What this course is about

Lean Healthcare is a practical approach to improving care delivery by removing waste, reducing delays, improving flow, strengthening reliability, and building a culture of continuous improvement. This course introduces the main Lean concepts healthcare organizations use to create safer and more efficient systems.

Why RCA matters

Healthcare systems face delays, handoff failures, duplicate work, clutter, variation, and workflow barriers that affect both staff and patients. Lean helps teams identify these problems clearly and improve them in a structured way.

What learners gain

Learners develop a foundational understanding of Lean thinking in healthcare, including waste reduction, value stream mapping, 5S, standard work, patient flow, visual management, problem solving, and sustainment.

Course style

Each lesson is short, clear, and structured for independent learning. Lessons include practical examples, professional design, and interactive knowledge checks with rationales.
